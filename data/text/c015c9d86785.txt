Scout Donation Platform Rebuilding Together: Scout-led earthquake response in Panama﻿ Donate Today On 9 October 2026, a magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck Panama, with its epicentre near Pitaloza Arriba on the Azuero Peninsula. The earthquake and subsequent aftershocks damaged homes, schools and community infrastructure, particularly in Los Santos, Herrera, Veraguas and Coclé. Scouts of Panama are organising a response to support affected families, working in coordination with the National Civil Protection System (SINAPROC), the Emergency Operations Centre (COE) and local authorities. Through this emergency appeal, we aim to raise funds to provide essential humanitarian supplies, support the recovery of schools and community spaces, and strengthen emergency preparedness. Activities Funds raised through this appeal will enable Scouts of Panama to mobilise volunteers and support humanitarian relief and recovery according to the needs identified on the ground. Priority actions include: - Distribution of essential relief supplies, including non-perishable food, family hygiene kits and water purification tablets or filters. - Volunteer clean-up activities and minor, non-structural repairs in schools and community spaces, following safety assessments and guidance from the responsible authorities. - Provision of basic community emergency equipment, including megaphones and first-aid kits. - Practical workshops on family emergency planning, responding to aftershocks and community resilience. — Los fondos recaudados a través de esta campaña permitirán a Scouts de Panamá movilizar voluntarios y apoyar acciones humanitarias

y de recuperación de acuerdo con las necesidades identificadas en el territorio. Las acciones prioritarias incluyen: - Distribución de suministros esenciales, incluyendo alimentos no perecederos, kits de higiene familiar y pastillas o filtros para potabilizar el agua. - Jornadas de limpieza y reparaciones menores no estructurales en escuelas y espacios comunitarios, siguiendo las evaluaciones de seguridad y las indicaciones de las autoridades responsables. - Dotación de equipos básicos para la respuesta comunitaria ante emergencias, incluyendo megáfonos y botiquines. - Talleres prácticos sobre preparación familiar ante emergencias, respuesta a réplicas y resiliencia comunitaria.﻿ Impact Your contribution will help Scouts of Panama provide direct support to families and communities affected by the earthquake. Scouts will work alongside communities to identify their needs and priorities, focusing on vulnerable rural areas of Los Santos, Herrera and Veraguas. Funds raised will contribute to immediate humanitarian assistance by improving access to food, safe drinking water and hygiene supplies. Scout-led recovery activities will also support safer schools and community spaces, while practical workshops will help families prepare for future emergencies. Together, we can help communities meet urgent needs, rebuild confidence and strengthen their capacity to respond to disasters. — T
