Volunteer: The 44th World Scout Conference What's in it for me? Volunteering at the 44th World Scout Conference is a unique opportunity to: Meet Scout Leaders from across the world Build international friendships Develop new skills and experiences Be part of a historic moment for Scouting Represent the spirit of UK Scouts and the global Scout Movement Whether you are an experienced event volunteer or attending your first international Scout event, there will be opportunities to get involved. How can I get involved? Registrations for volunteer roles will open on November 29th 2027, one year before the start of the World Scout Conference. You can register your interest now.
