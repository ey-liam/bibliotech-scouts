Every Drop Matters: Safe Water for Displaced Families in Benue, Nigeria ﻿ Donate to this Project ﻿ Imagine walking up to 3 km every day just to find water and still not knowing if it is safe to drink. Displaced families and children in Zaki-Biam, Benue State, face this reality every day, increasing their exposure to waterborne diseases such as cholera, typhoid and dysentery. Every Drop Matters will bring safe water closer to the IDP camp, helping protect families from preventable illness while reducing the hours children spend searching for water instead of learning. With your support, every drop can mean health, dignity, education, and hope. ﻿ health 501-2000 People Impacted africa The problem﻿ In Zaki-Biam IDP Camp, displaced families lack access to safe and clean drinking water. Children and women walk long distances daily to fetch water from unsafe streams, exposing them to waterborne diseases such as cholera, typhoid, and dysentery. These dangerous journeys also increase the risk of harassment and abuse, especially for young girls and women. With little or no income, families cannot afford to purchase safe water, leaving them vulnerable to preventable illness and suffer

ing. The absence of clean water has led to frequent sickness, poor hygiene, and increased health risks within the camp. Children miss school due to illness, families spend scarce resources on medical treatment, and women live in constant fear while searching for water. Every Drop Matters: Safe Water for Displaced Families in Benue, Nigeria 8 months ago Our solution Impact This project will provide safe and clean water to children and families in Zaki-Biam IDP Camp, directly improving their health and reducing the risk of waterborne diseases such as cholera, typhoid, and dysentery. It will reduce the daily burden of traveling long distances for water, protecting young girls and women from the risk of harassment and abuse. By giving the community reliable access to clean water, the project will enhance hygiene, restore dignity, and create a safer, healthier environment. Ultimately, it will empower the community, giving them hope and the ability to focus on education, work, and rebuilding their l

ives. Key Project Activities Community Engagement and Planning: We will engage with both the IDP and host communities to discuss the project, raise awareness, and gather input on the most suitable and safe location for the borehole. Borehole Drilling and Installation: We will drill a borehole to provide a sustainable source of clean water and install pumps, storage tanks, and taps to ensure easy access for the community. Water Treatment Materials: We will provide necessary water treatment materials, including filters, chemicals, and water testing kits, to guarantee that the water is safe for drinking and household use. Education and Training: We will conduct workshops on hygiene, sanitation, and safe water handling, training community members to operate and maintain the water supply system effectively. Operations and Maintenance: We will support ongoing labor, transportation, and monitoring to ensure that the borehole remains functional and continues to serve the community sustainably.﻿ This project has been endorsed by the National Scout Organization ﻿ Donate to this Project ﻿ Making a difference for health﻿.
