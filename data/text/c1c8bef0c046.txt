Grants can support a range of projects that are addressing global issues and trends that affect young people. Messengers of Peace Support Fund The Messengers of Peace Fund provides financial support to Scouts so that they can take action and contribute to peace and sustainable development in their communities. The funds supports projects involved in capacity strengthening, inspiring Messengers of Peace, disaster response, environment, peace and culture of dialogue, and special projects.
