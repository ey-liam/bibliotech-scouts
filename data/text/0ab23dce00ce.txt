How we work Our values shape how we work together and how we support the Scout Movement. At the World Scout Bureau, staff and volunteers bring teamwork, creativity, and openness to the way we approach our work. We are always learning and aim to put the values at the heart of Scouting into practice: being respectful, people-centred, impactful, and collaborative.
