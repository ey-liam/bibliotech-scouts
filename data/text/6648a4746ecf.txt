Here are some tips to capture stand-out photos and videos of key places, actions and people in your project with your smartphone. Every photo and video clip should have a main person, action or object. Whichever it is, make sure it gets attention by keeping it in the foreground and in focus. Double check there isn’t anything in the background that you wouldn’t want in shot. Steady your phone by placing it on a tripod, shelf or any stable place. If you're holding it, keep it close to your body to avoid shaking. Video tip: Film horizontally for YouTube and vertically for Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. Show what your team is doing and share the enthusiasm via expressions and action shots. Avoid posed photos. Video tip: To gain support, try speaking directly to the camera.
