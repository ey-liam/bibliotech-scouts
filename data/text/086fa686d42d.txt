Find out more About the Congress The World Scout Education Congress is the leading global platform for Scout educators, formal and non-formal education experts, and other non-formal education actors to come together to exchange knowledge, share research, and explore new approaches to education, with a strong focus on non-formal learning. Held every few years, the Congress brings together National Scout Organization leadership, educators, and partners from across the world to help shape the future of education in Scouting. It inspires the development of innovative educational programmes and initiatives that respond to the evolving needs of young people and future generations. The 5th World Scout Education Congress in 2026 will offer a global space for dialogue on education in Scouting, with a particular focus on safeguarding, innovation in education , youth leadership, and voluntee

ring, key strategic priorities outlined in the Strategy for Scouting. Programme Overview The 5th World Education Congress will showcase how Scouting is shaping the future of education worldwide by spotlighting youth-led leadership and innovation, reinforcing safeguarding as a cornerstone of high-quality learning environments, and exploring modern educational approaches that build essential competencies for today’s and tomorrow’s world. The programme will also present new ways to engage and support adults in Scouting education, fostering strong intergenerational collaboration and delivering impactful, transformative learning experiences. About Singapore Singapore is an island country and a vibrant city-state located at the heart of Southeast Asia, known for its rich multicultural heritage, modern skyline, and seamless blend of tradition and innovation. Also known as the Little Red Dot, Singapore is globally connected and consistently recognised for its safety, efficiency, and world-class infrastructure. Singapore is highly accessible for international visitors, with Changi Airport serving as a major global aviation hub and offering excellent connectivity to more than 50+ countries and 160+ cities worldwide. Visitors from most countries are eligible for visa-free entry for short-term stays, typically ranging from 30 to 90 days, depending on nationality. However, visa requirements vary, and some travellers may be required to apply for a visa prior to arrival.
