World Scout Foundation World Baden-Powell Fellowship We believe that lasting positive change in the world happens when we empower young people through Scouting. Join thousands of other BP Fellows from 86 countries and territories across the world. United in global Fellowship. The Baden-Powell Fellowship is a global network of passionate and dedicated individual supporters of Scouting, "BP Fellows", who give their time and resources and lend their name and networks, to help young people to create a better world, through Scouting. The World Scout Foundation brings BP Fellows together at online and in-person events and works hard to share news of Scouting's impact, recognise the valuable contribution of all BP Fellows and welcome new members, who are personally awarded a s

pecial insignia by His Royal Highness the Grand Duke of Luxembourg, WSF Honorary Chair. The BP Fellowship ensures a lifetime commitment to World Scouting and a link to others who share the same ideals. Joining and participating, especially when you have been a Scout—although this is not a requirement—offers the greatest reward in being involved in the development of young people around the world. JOIN THE BP FELLOWSHIP The BP Fellowship has different donation levels. We welcome you as a BP Fellow with a minimum pledged donation of USD 10,000, while those under 35 can become Young BP Fellows with a USD 1,000 donation. You can donate all at once or in smaller payments up to ten years. To join as a BP Fellow, simply make a donation on this page and fill out the pledge form that follows.
