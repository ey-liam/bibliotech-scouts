Scouts around the world connect, explore, and take action at JOTA-JOTI 2026 | WOSM Skip to main content Scouts around the world connect, explore, and take action at JOTA-JOTI 2026 Youth Engagement Partnerships 21 October 2026 3 minutes From 16 to 18 October, Scouts from every corner of the globe came together for JOTA-JOTI, the world's largest digital and radio Scout event. United by the theme, A Peaceful and Inclusive World, young people connected online and over the airwaves to learn, share experiences, build friendships, and take action for a better world. Over three days, more than 720,000 Scouts from 170 National Scout Organizations registered to join activities through amateur radio, online platforms and local Scout gatherings, showing how Scouting can bring people together across borders. One highlight of the weekend was Hike the Planet , which encouraged Scouts to get outdoors and contribute towards a collective journey around the Earth's circumference. Scout surpassed the global challenge target, logging over 47,100 kilometres in walks, hikes, and community activities. Copyright Yassine Elhor Turning ideas into action was another important part of this year's event. Through the Changemaker Challenge , Scouts explored how they could help create more peaceful and inclusive communities. By the close of JOTA-JOTI 2026, close to 36,700 Scouts had made a Changemaker Challeng

e pledge, committing to make a positive difference in their schools, Scout groups, and local communities. Meanwhile, over 1,000 Scouts also logged on to the JOTA-JOTI Minecraft experience, building virtual worlds and campsites, and visiting the recreation of Switzerland’s Kandersteg International Scout Centre (KISC). The programme offered plenty of other opportunities to learn, connect, and have fun, with activities, workshops, and challenges covering everything from global citizenship to community action. A special session with NASA astronaut and Eagle Scout Mike Fossum gave participants a glimpse into life aboard the International Space Station and how people from different countries, cultures, and backgrounds work together towards a common goal. Scouts also heard from US human rights activist Martin Luther King III, who joined a webinar to discuss youth leadership, global friendship, and the importance of working together to create positive change. Copyright Tily Analamanga Andrefana In Challenge Valley, thousands of participants completed activities, collected challenge codes, and earned digital badges while discovering new experiences across the JOTA-JOTI platform. Two JOTA-JOTI Live shows, hosted by Ruhi Jahan (Bangladesh Scouts) and Ryan Harrison (Scouts Australia), brought even more of the global Scout community together through stories, games, special guests, and showc

ases from Scouts around the world. World Scouting thanks the volunteers, Scout leaders, National Scout Organizations, and partners who helped make this 69th edition of JOTA-JOTI possible. Special thanks go to Accenture, SAP, and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, in partnership with World Scouting and the World Scout Foundation through the Messengers of Peace Fund, for their continued support in empowering young people through online learning and technology.
