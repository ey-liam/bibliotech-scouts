This flagship initiative enables young people to lead projects that promote lasting tolerance, understanding, and sustainable development. With over 16 million projects completed and counting, Scouts continue to create meaningful impact worldwide. With funding from the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Scouts are working with local communities, key organisations, and strategic global partners to build a culture of dialogue for mutual understanding. Every project — from small actions to large-scale community initiatives — brings us closer to achieving our shared vision of a more peaceful and sustainable future, equipping young people with the skills to make a difference and be ready for life. 2010 Messengers of Peace launched 16 million+ Projects and actions Nearly 3 billion Community service hours towards the Sustainable Development Goals A peaceful and sustainable f

uture Messengers of Peace empowers Scouts around the world to create positive change, directly supporting the Sustainable Development Goals. Through service projects that promote community service, leadership, and safeguarding, Scouts are tackling some of the biggest global challenges facing our world today. Whether it’s leading peace-building activities, advocating for gender equality, or supporting refugees, Messengers of Peace inspires young people to be active citizens and agents of positive change.
