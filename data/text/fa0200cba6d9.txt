Youth Representatives | WOSM Skip to main content Youth Representatives World Scouting’s Youth Representatives are young leaders from across the Movement who bring the voices, ideas, and experiences of Scouts into global and regional advocacy spaces. Through their work, they help World Scouting engage on issues that matter to young people, contribute to policy discussions, and strengthen meaningful youth participation in decision-making.
