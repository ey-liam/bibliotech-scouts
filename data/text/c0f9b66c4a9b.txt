Registration – 5th World Scout Education Congress Skip to content FR EN Registration Now Open How to Register The World Scout Education Congress is a unique opportunity to connect with people who are shaping the future of Scouting education around the world. Join education leaders, volunteers, youth leaders, researchers, and practitioners to exchange ideas, explore emerging trends, share practical solutions, and learn from innovative approaches being implemented across different contexts. The deadline to register has been extended to 31 October 2026. Who should attend? National Scout Organizations are encouraged to invite participation from: Education, Youth Programme and Volunteering leads Safeguarding and youth protection officers Youth leaders Young adults shaping educational experiences Decision-makers responsible for educational strategies and frameworks. How to register Registration takes place through a nomination process managed by National Scout Organizations: The International Commissioner or official NSO contact submits a pre-registration form with the names of nominated participants. Each nominated participant receives a personalised registration link. Participants complete their personal registration. The NSO receives the invoice for payment of all its nominated participants. All participants must: Be endorsed by their National Scout Organization. Complete the mand

atory Safe from Harm online learning course before attending. What's included? Your participation fee includes: Access to all Congress sessions and activities Lunch and coffee breaks throughout the event Two evening social events The fee does not include: Accommodation Breakfast Airport transfers Transport from the accommodations to the venue When registering, please ensure that all information is accurate and complete, including dietary requirements, accessibility or support needs, and emergency contact details.
