Summer is 'high season' at the World Scout Centre when most Scouts visit from around the world and take part in different activities at the Permanent Mini Jamboree. Summer Short-Term Staff are divided into three key teams: Catering/Shop: During the summer season, the catering team serves about 50,000 delicious meals to our staff and guests. They work alongside the catering long-term staff daily, l

earning how the kitchen works, how to cook for a high number of people, keep up with the Swiss hygiene and food safety standards, all while working in an international team of volunteers and providing all scouts visiting an amazing catering experience. In our shop, the staff meets guests from all around the world and work on the till, surrounded by all the coolest memorabilia and merchandise of the Permanent Mini Jamboree. Campsite/House: The campsite/house team is responsible for the smooth running of the campsite and our buildings over the summer. Working at the campsite, Short Term Staff are in charge of many tasks: from checking guests into their site to daily cleaning of the facilities, managing equipment rentals and overseeing the recycling Centre. There is always work to be done on the grounds and managing the campsite office to provide a point of contact for the campsite guests. The House team looks after the checking in our guests in our buildings, preparing rooms, daily cleaning, laundry and providing a great guest experience. This team loves funny hats, chatting around the folding table and listening to epic playlists! Programme: Our summer programme team is broken up into three main areas: Activity guides who run all the workshops and some hikes, Hike guides who provide guided hiking to our guests and Climbing guides who provide a climbing experience in the Alps. Al

l programme guides work together and support in other areas when needed to ensure that all our guests have a fantastic and memorable experience while taking part in the programme over the summer. On the days where there are less Programme Bookings, you can expect to help out in the other work areas such as the kitchen or campsite. If you want to have an unforgettable summer, apply to be a Pinkie and part of a team of 110 volunteers leading the World Scout Centre! They are Scouts and Guides who come to work to Kanders

teg for a season of 3 months . A new team of Short Term staff arrives every June, September, December and March. Being a Short Term member of Staff in KISC is a unique experience. As a Short Term Staff, you will have the time of your life; growing, learning and having fun. The Long-Term Staff are the link between the seasons and the different Short Term Staff teams. Many of them started by working here as Short Term Staff and chose to return to work for a longer time. Some work here for 4 months , some for 9 months to 12 months . All Long Term Staff are doing specific jobs in different work areas like Reception, Programme, Catering, Administration & Finance, Maintenance etc. In summer, there are about 35 Long Term Staff, and during the rest of the year, we have about 25. Of course, there has to be somebody in charge! We have a Director who is employed by the KISC Association to run the Centre strategically. The Director is supported by the Senior Leadership Team, who are working here for 2 to 3 years . The Senior Leadership Team consists of a Guest Services Director, Communications, Partnerships and Fundraising Director, Infrastructure Director, Programme Director and Staff Development Director. Helpers are usually ex-staff who come back for a short period, from a couple of days and up to two weeks. They come with fresh energy, and provide the greatest support to the seasonal s

taff team during special events, or busy weeks. High Season Staff are Ex-Staff members who come back to help the Pinkie Team during a busy times at the Centre usually for a 6 weeks period over the peak of the summer season. Remote Volunteers are ex-staff members who help the centre from a distance, in a range of operational and strategic tasks, that require a specific set of skills and qualifications. These roles are usually for a fixed term, starting from six months, and up to two years. Want to apply?
