Every Drop Matters: Safe Water for Displaced Families in Benue, Nigeria active Imagine walking up to 3 km every day just to find water and still not knowing if it is safe to drink. Displaced families and children in Zaki-Biam, Benue State, face this reality every day, increasing their exposure to waterborne diseases such as cholera, typhoid and dysentery. Every Drop Matters will bring safe water closer to the IDP camp, helping protect families from preventable illness while reducing the hours children spend searching for water instead of learning. The World Scout Foundation ’s mission is to develop and strengthen the impact of Scouting worldwide by providing financial support and other resources to the World Organisation of the Scout Movement.
